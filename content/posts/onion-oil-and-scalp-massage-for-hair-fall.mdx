Scalp massage and onion oil are popular home remedies for hair fall. Here is an honest look.

## Does scalp massage help?

Gentle massage may improve blood flow and it feels relaxing, which can help with stress. Results on regrowth are modest.

## What about onion oil?

Small studies suggest onion extract may help some people, but strong evidence is limited. Do a patch test first, as it can irritate skin.

## How to massage your scalp

### Simple routine

1. Use your fingertips, not nails.
2. Make small circles for 5 minutes.
3. Do it 3 to 4 times a week.

## Be realistic

- Give any routine at least 3 months.
- Keep your diet and sleep in check.
- Stop if you notice itching or redness.

<Callout>If hair fall continues, speak to a dermatologist rather than trying more products.</Callout>
