Losing 50 to 100 hairs a day is normal. If you notice much more than that, one of these causes may be behind it.

## 1. Stress

High stress can push hair into its shedding phase a few months later.

## 2. Low iron or protein

### Check your diet

Eggs, lentils, leafy greens and nuts support healthy hair growth.

## 3. Hormonal changes

Pregnancy, postpartum, thyroid issues and PCOS can all affect hair.

## 4. Harsh styling

- Tight ponytails and braids
- Frequent bleaching
- Daily high-heat tools

## 5. Scalp problems

Dandruff and scalp infections can weaken hair at the root.

## 6. Crash dieting and illness

Sudden weight loss or a high fever can cause temporary shedding.

## 7. Genetics

Pattern hair loss runs in families and needs a doctor's guidance.

<Callout>See a doctor if hair loss is sudden, patchy or comes with scalp pain or itching.</Callout>
