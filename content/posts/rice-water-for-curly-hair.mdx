Rice water is a simple, cheap rinse many people with curls swear by. Here is how to try it without overdoing it.

## What is rice water?

Rice water is the cloudy liquid left after soaking or boiling rice. It contains starches and a small amount of amino acids, which can leave hair feeling smoother and slightly stronger.

## How to make it

1. Rinse half a cup of rice.
2. Soak it in two cups of water for 30 minutes.
3. Strain the water into a clean bottle.
4. Use it within 24 hours (keep it in the fridge).

## How to use it

### Step by step

- Shampoo as normal.
- Pour rice water over your hair and scalp.
- Leave for 5 minutes, then rinse with cool water.

<Callout>Tip: Use it once a week at most. Too much protein can make curls feel stiff and dry.</Callout>

## Who should be careful?

If your hair already feels stiff, brittle or straw-like, skip rice water and focus on moisture first. Always do a small patch test if you have a sensitive scalp.

## Final thoughts

Rice water will not work miracles, but as a weekly rinse it can add softness and shine. Pair it with a good conditioner for the best result.
