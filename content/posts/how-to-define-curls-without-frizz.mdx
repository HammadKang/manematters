Frizz usually comes from lost moisture and too much touching. This routine keeps curls defined and soft.

## Start with the right wash

Use a gentle, sulfate-free shampoo on your scalp only. Let the suds run down the lengths.

## Condition generously

### Detangle while wet

Apply conditioner and detangle with your fingers or a wide-tooth comb, starting from the ends.

### Do not rinse everything out

Leaving a little conditioner in is a good way to lock in moisture.

## Style on soaking wet hair

- Apply a curl cream, then a light gel.
- Scrunch upwards towards the scalp.
- Do not touch until fully dry.

## Dry gently

Use a cotton t-shirt or microfiber towel instead of a rough towel, and diffuse on low heat if you are in a hurry.

<Callout>Once dry, gently scrunch out the crunch to reveal soft, defined curls.</Callout>
