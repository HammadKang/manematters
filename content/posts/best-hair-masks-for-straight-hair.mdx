You do not need expensive products for soft hair. Try one of these once a week.

## 1. Banana and honey mask

Mash one ripe banana with a spoon of honey. Apply to the lengths for 20 minutes, then rinse well.

## 2. Yogurt and aloe mask

Mix three spoons of plain yogurt with one spoon of aloe vera gel. Leave on for 15 minutes.

## 3. Light coconut oil treatment

### How to apply

Warm a teaspoon of coconut oil, apply to the ends only, and leave for 30 minutes before shampooing.

## Tips

- Always rinse thoroughly so no residue is left.
- Patch test first if you have allergies.
- Do not use more than once a week.

<Callout>Fine hair? Skip the oil mask and choose yogurt and aloe instead.</Callout>
