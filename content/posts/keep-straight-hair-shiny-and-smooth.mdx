Straight hair shows damage and oil quickly, so small habits make a big difference.

## Why straight hair looks dull

Natural oils travel down straight hair easily, which can make roots greasy and ends dry at the same time.

## Wash smart

### Focus on the scalp

Shampoo the roots, condition only mid-length to ends.

### Finish cool

A quick cool rinse helps smooth the hair cuticle and adds shine.

## Easy shine habits

- Use a silk pillowcase to reduce friction.
- Brush gently, starting from the ends.
- Apply heat protectant before any hot tool.
- Trim every 8 to 12 weeks.

## Weekly care

A light hair mask once a week keeps ends soft without weighing hair down.

<Callout>Avoid heavy oils on the roots. A few drops on the ends are enough.</Callout>
